\section{Introduction}
This chapter presents Sprint 5, focused on Project Manager Supervision and Attendance Monitoring. The Project Manager has access to three pages: \textbf{Projects}, \textbf{Members}, and \textbf{Members Attendance}. Access is strictly limited to projects assigned by the Administrator and members belonging to those projects.
