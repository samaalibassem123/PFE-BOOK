\section{Introduction}
This chapter presents the Project Manager modules, focused on project-scope supervision and manager-level operational monitoring. The Project Manager has access to three pages: \textbf{Projects}, \textbf{Members}, and \textbf{Members Attendance}. Access is strictly limited to projects assigned by the Administrator and members belonging to those projects.
