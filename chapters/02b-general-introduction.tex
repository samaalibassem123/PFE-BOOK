This final year project was carried out at OneTech Business Solutions (OTBS) as part of the requirements for obtaining the Bachelor's Degree in Business Intelligence. It was completed in a professional environment where attendance monitoring, HR follow-up, and project management rely on clear, reliable, and well-structured information systems.

The starting point of this work was a real operational need within the company. OTBS already relied on two separate platforms: \textbf{BioTime}, which was used for biometric attendance tracking, and \textbf{EasyProject}, which was used for HR and project management. Although each platform was useful within its own scope, they were not directly connected. As a result, attendance follow-up was not always consistent, especially when employees were working on external missions and therefore appeared active in projects without appearing in office attendance records.

To address this issue, the project aimed to design and implement a unified solution capable of integrating data from both systems into a centralized database and a dedicated web application. The proposed platform was designed to support several user roles, including administrators, HR staff, and project managers, while also ensuring secure access, data consistency, and easier reporting.

From a technical perspective, this work covered the main stages of a complete full-stack project. It included the analysis of existing systems, the design of the target architecture, the implementation of ETL pipelines, the development of a FastAPI backend, the creation of a React frontend, and the integration of role-based authentication with dedicated business modules. The project also explored the integration of an RH Agent in order to improve user interaction and support daily operational tasks.

This report presents the work carried out throughout the internship. It begins with the general framework of the project, then moves to the analysis and design phase, the ETL and unified database implementation, the authentication and administration modules, the HR and Project Manager features, and finally the integration of the RH Agent.
